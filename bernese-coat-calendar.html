    How the plan is built

    The plan runs for 365 days from your start date. On your brushing days you get one weekly-routine session. Inside a shedding season the routine pauses and you brush daily, or every other day if you chose that. Each season starts on the first day of the month you picked.

    The file holds three kinds of events: the weekly routine as one repeating event, each shedding season as one repeating event, and an all-day bar that marks each season in your month view. Delete the series and the whole plan is gone.

    The file is built in this browser. Nothing is sent to us, there is no account, and nothing is stored.

    Add the file to your calendar

    • Google Calendar on a computer: Settings, then Import & export, then Import. Pick the file and the calendar it should go into.
    • Apple Calendar on a Mac: File, then Import, then choose the file.
    • iPhone or iPad: open the downloaded file and tap Add All.
    • Other apps: look for Import or Open calendar file.

    Tip: import into a new, separate calendar. You can then hide or delete the whole plan in one step.

    What happens in a session

    Brush through the coat section by section. The AKC suggests working tangles out with a slicker brush or metal comb. Hill’s points to the areas behind the ears and around the hindquarters as the places to watch for mats. For what to ask a groomer, see the groomer instructions guide.

    Where the advice comes from

    • AKC: Bernese Mountain Dog grooming (weekly, daily in shedding season)
    • Hill’s breed guide (two to three times a week, spring and fall shedding)
